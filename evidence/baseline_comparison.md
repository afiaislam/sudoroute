## Definitions

- **Bank wire**: SWIFT wire from an authorised bank to the account printed on the invoice. Legal, no invoice check, ignores timing.
- **Cheapest-fee assistant**: Licensed channels only; lowest cost; pays the account on the invoice; does not check country rules, university partner status, limits or arrival time; executes every request.
- **scams_that_reach_a_fraudster**: Of 42 scam invoices, how many send money to an account that is not the university's verified one.
- **rule_violations**: Of 160 payments, how many use a channel the home-country rules, the university or the channel limit do not allow.
- **late_rate_pct**: Expected share of payments arriving after the real due date (channel delivery window and reliability).
- **avoidable_late_pct**: Lateness that an allowed channel could have avoided. Some deadlines are too close for any allowed channel; SudoRoute warns about these early with a pay-by date.
- **overpayments_on_retry**: Of 3 retry situations, how many end with the university paid more than the invoice.
- **why_assistant_cheaper**: The assistant is cheaper and faster only because it uses channels the rules do not allow; SudoRoute chooses among allowed channels only.
- **note**: SudoRoute sends genuine invoices flagged for a check to the student first; it never pays the account printed on an invoice.

24 of 160 payments have a deadline too close for any allowed channel; these count as late for every legal system.

## Retry cases (real agent)

- Same request sent twice: SudoRoute overpaid 0 MYR; a baseline that repeats the payment overpays 18500.0 MYR.
- Transfer returned by the bank: SudoRoute overpaid 0.0 MYR; a baseline that repeats the payment overpays 0.0 MYR.
- University received less than sent: SudoRoute overpaid 0 MYR; a baseline that repeats the payment overpays 18357.15 MYR.

Why the assistant looks cheaper and faster: 120 of its 160 payments use a channel that the home-country rules, the university or the channel limit do not allow (for example a debit card above its limit, or a money-transfer operator a country does not permit for tuition). SudoRoute only chooses among allowed channels.

Limitations: sandbox fees, markups and delivery times are illustrative; the hold-out invoices are synthetic. The genuine invoices SudoRoute sends for a check are a real cost: the student has to confirm with the university first.
