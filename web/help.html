Getting started

What is the fastest way to try SudoRoute?

Press Try it now — 1 click on the home page. You get a private sandbox with a student (Md Soron) and his mother (Mst Nishi). The Judge mode panel walks you through a full payment in about a minute: ask the agent → approve as the mother → confirm as the student → fast-forward 7 days → certificate.

Is any real money involved?

No. Everything is a sandbox: universities, banks, wallets and transfers are simulated. Exchange-rate levels are real (a live feed or a dated snapshot, always labelled); their future movement is simulated.

How do I create a real account instead?

Choose Get started, pick Student or Guardian, and type your university. Verified universities can be paid straight away; others are added as pending verification and can't receive money until their bank account is confirmed with the university itself.

Which languages are supported?

The interface is in English, 简体中文 and বাংলা. The assistant also understands Malay, and the scam check reads invoices in all four languages.

Paying tuition

How does SudoRoute choose the channel?

It compares bank wires, education payment platforms and licensed money transfer operators on landed cost: the hidden exchange-rate markup, fees and the priced risk of arriving late. Channels your home country or university doesn't allow — and informal ones like hundi — are excluded even when cheaper.

What is smart timing?

Ask “Pay at the best rate before my deadline”. You approve once; the agent then checks the rate every day and pays by itself as soon as it reaches a target — or on the last safe day, so it still arrives on time. It never pays below a price floor you signed; if the rate falls further it pauses and asks you.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, if your university allows it. After one approval the later installments run on their dates — same payee and invoice only — and pause automatically if your currency weakens more than 5%.

What happens if a transfer comes back or arrives short?

SudoRoute explains why, refuses a blind resend, and — if an intermediary bank took a fee — offers to pay only the shortfall. The overpayment check makes paying twice impossible.

What does SudoRoute cost?

One visible service fee: 0.5% of the payment, at least USD 3 and at most USD 25 — shown on every quote and waived during the pilot. We never add a hidden exchange-rate markup. See Pricing.

Security & codes

What is the 6-digit code?

It proves that you — not the AI and not someone in your account — approve this exact payment. In the sandbox it arrives on the phone icon at the top of the portal. You can link an authenticator app instead under Security.

How do I link Google or Microsoft Authenticator?

Open Security → Authenticator app, enter your password, scan the QR code and type the code the app shows. From then on only the app's code approves payments; text-message codes stop working.

What does the kill switch do?

One press cancels pending payments and stops installment and smart-timing plans. Nothing can be prepared or paid until it is released with your password. If your guardian switched it on, only a guardian can release it.

How do I know the security claims are true?

Run the Security Lab: it fires 57 real attacks — including a deliberately compromised AI model — at a live copy of SudoRoute and shows each result.

Guardians & family

How does a parent get linked?

The student enters the parent's name and e-mail at sign-up; when the parent registers as a Guardian with that e-mail, they are linked automatically. A one-time link code works too (Security → Create a guardian link code).

Why does my guardian have to approve?

When family money pays the fees, a linked guardian approves with their own code before the student confirms. A decline is final. Turning this off needs the student's password.

Can a guardian send money to the student?

Yes — under Send funds in the guardian portal (sandbox limit about USD 10,000 a day).

Certificates & proof

What is a payment certificate?

A signed record (Ed25519) of an on-time tuition payment or of your payment history, which a landlord, bank or scholarship office can check on /verify without an account. Change one character and it fails.

What does “anchored on Bitcoin” mean?

A 32-byte fingerprint of each certificate and of the audit log is timestamped on the Bitcoin blockchain via OpenTimestamps. No money and no personal data go on-chain. Anyone can download the proof and check it at opentimestamps.org.

What is proof of funds?

A signed statement that you held at least an amount on a date — the real balance is never written into it. SudoRoute refuses to sign a claim your wallets can't back.

The AI agent

What can the AI do on its own?

Plan multi-step tasks (check an invoice, compare channels, price currency risk), prepare a signed payment for you to approve, and — after you approve a bounded plan — execute installments or a smart-timing payment within the limits you signed.

What can it never do?

Move money without your code, pay an account typed in a message, use crypto or hundi, raise limits, delete logs, or follow instructions hidden in an e-mail. These rules are enforced in code, not by asking the model nicely. AI proposes, code disposes, humans execute.

What is the AI auditor?

An independent AI that reviews every prepared payment and every payment the agent is about to make by itself. It can only put a payment on hold with a reason; you read it and decide. It can never approve anything.

SudoRoute is a prototype built for the 2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition (International Track, Topic C). It is not a bank or a licensed money transmitter and it moves no real money: every account, university and transfer on this site is simulated. In production, payments would be executed only by licensed partners — banks, education payment platforms and licensed money transfer operators — under their licences in each country, and SudoRoute would charge one visible service fee, never a hidden exchange-rate markup.
