Every payment stops at the same five checkpoints

You can watch each one happen. Nothing leaves your account before the last stop, and the agent can't skip a stop on its own.

  1. Invoice checked

    Sender, bank account, amount and wording are compared with the university's verified record, in English, Malay, Bangla or Chinese.

  2. Route chosen

    Bank wire, education platforms and licensed transfer services are compared on real cost, including the hidden exchange-rate markup. Illegal channels are excluded even when cheaper.

  3. Currency risk priced

    4,000 simulated futures show how likely waiting would push the bill over your family's budget, and when instalments help.

  4. Guardian approved

    When family money is involved, a linked parent approves with their own code first. Either of you can hit the kill switch.

  5. Student confirmed

    You confirm with a one-time code. The payment is signed, so nobody can change the payee or amount after you approve.

Fake tuition invoices stop here

Scammers copy a university's name, change one letter in the web address, say the bank details have changed and demand payment within hours. Some now hide instructions meant for AI assistants. This one scored 100 out of 100.

Questions

Short answers to what students and parents ask first.

Is any real money moved?

No. This is a sandbox built for the competition. Universities, banks and balances are simulated; exchange rates are real when the server can reach the live feed, and every screen says whether they are LIVE or a dated REFERENCE snapshot.

Can the AI pay someone by itself?

No. It can prepare a signed proposal. Money moves only through a separate confirmation step that needs your one-time code, and your guardian's when they are linked. Instructions inside invoices or e-mails are never followed.

My university isn't in the list. Can I still use SudoRoute?

Yes. Type its name when you sign up. It is added as "waiting for verification": the assistant can check invoices for scams straight away, and payments open once the university's official bank account has been confirmed with the university itself.

Why was a cheaper option excluded?

Some channels are illegal for tuition (such as hundi) or not allowed by your home country's rules. SudoRoute shows them so you can see the price difference, explains why they were excluded, and never uses them.

What does my guardian see?

Linked guardians see your invoices, payments and balances, approve payments and can freeze them. They never see your password, codes or chat with the assistant, and phone numbers are masked.

What is a payment certificate?

A short signed text that proves a tuition payment arrived on time. You choose when to create one. Anyone can check it on the verify page without an account, which helps when you have no local credit history yet.
