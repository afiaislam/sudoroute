Terms of use

SudoRoute is a prototype built for the 2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition. It is a sandbox: universities, banks, wallets and transfers are simulated, and no real money moves.

  • SudoRoute is not a bank, payment institution or licensed money transmitter. Nothing on this site is financial or legal advice.
  • Country rule packs, fees and delivery times are simplified illustrations and must be validated with regulators and licensed partners before any real use.
  • Judge-mode sandboxes and their accounts are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Please don't use the site to attack other people's accounts. Testing your own sandbox — or using the Security Lab — is welcome.

Privacy

  • What we store: your name, e-mail, role, a salted PBKDF2 hash of your password (never the password), your phone number (shown masked to others), your sandbox wallet and payment history, and an append-only audit log with personal data redacted.
  • What we never store: card numbers, bank passwords or one-time codes. Codes are derived per payment and never written down.
  • AI: when Gemini is connected, it receives only what each task needs — never your password, codes or full account numbers. Pasted e-mails are read only by a quarantined model with no tools.
  • Newsletter and contact: your e-mail is used only to send the newsletter or reply to you. Ask us and we delete it.
  • On-chain: only a 32-byte fingerprint goes on the Bitcoin blockchain — no names, amounts or account data.
  • Cookies: one session cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict) and your language/theme choice in your browser. No trackers or ads.

Responsible security disclosure

Found a vulnerability? Thank you. Please e-mail afiaislam2313@gmail.com with steps to reproduce. Use your own judge sandbox, don't access other people's data, and give us a reasonable time to fix it before you publish. You can also see every defence we test in the Security Lab.

In production, payments would be executed only by licensed partners — banks, education payment platforms and licensed money transfer operators — under their licences in each country, and SudoRoute would charge one visible service fee, never a hidden exchange-rate markup.
