How SudoRoute makes money — and how it never does

✅ We earn

a visible service fee per payment (0.5%, capped at USD 25), an optional reconciliation plan for universities, and a disclosed referral share from licensed partners.

🚫 We never earn

from a hidden exchange-rate markup, from selling your data, or from steering you to a channel that pays us more. The route ranking ignores partner revenue entirely.

📈 Why it can work

Millions of international students pay tuition every term. A family saving about 1.5% on a USD 6,000 payment keeps ~USD 90 — and pays us at most USD 25 of it. Universities save time and see fewer scam victims.

⚖️ Compliance first

Money is moved only by licensed partners. SudoRoute is the agent and the safety layer: verified payees, signed approvals, audit trail and limits.

SudoRoute is a prototype built for the 2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition (International Track, Topic C). It is not a bank or a licensed money transmitter and it moves no real money: every account, university and transfer on this site is simulated. In production, payments would be executed only by licensed partners — banks, education payment platforms and licensed money transfer operators — under their licences in each country, and SudoRoute would charge one visible service fee, never a hidden exchange-rate markup.
